Exciting news from the CIRCULAR FUELS project: our first Staff Exchange is officially under way. Prof.
Tomás Ramírez Reina, from the Surface Science & Catalysis laboratory ([SURFCAT](https://surfcat.icms.us-csic.es/)) at the
[Universidad de Sevilla](https://www.us.es/), started his secondment at GreenX Technology Ltd, hosted at the Surrey
Technology Centre in the Surrey Research Park, United Kingdom.

The aim of this first exchange is to bring the catalyst design expertise developed in Seville straight into an industrial
R&D environment. During his stay, Prof. Ramírez Reina is working alongside the GreenX team on catalyst optimisation and
process modelling, two of the company's main contributions to the project.

Beyond the technical work, exchanges like this one are the backbone of an MSCA Staff Exchange action: they strengthen the
ties between academia and industry, create new opportunities for collaboration, and help turn laboratory results into
technologies that can reach the market.
