CIRCULAR FUELS welcomes Gabriel De Freitas Batista from the University of Hohenheim (UHOH) to the University of Seville for a two-month research stay, marking the project's second staff exchange.

During his stay, Gabriel is exploring the potential of hydrochar materials obtained through hydrothermal carbonization (HTC) as supports for catalysts intended to convert hydrothermal liquefaction (HTL)-derived oils through aqueous-phase reforming (APR), with the aim of producing hydrogen.

This research brings together different hydrothermal conversion processes to investigate how carbon-rich materials obtained from HTC could be used in catalyst development for the conversion of HTL-derived oils. By exploring the potential of hydrochar-supported catalysts, the work seeks to contribute to more integrated approaches to biomass valorisation and sustainable hydrogen production.

The research reflects the circular approach at the heart of CIRCULAR FUELS: exploring the value of process-derived materials, improving resource utilisation and developing innovative pathways towards sustainable fuels and energy carriers.

Beyond the experimental work, Gabriel's stay provides an opportunity to exchange scientific knowledge, share laboratory expertise and strengthen collaboration between the University of Hohenheim and the University of Seville.
