On 22 October 2026, GREENE hosted the first CIRCULAR FUELS workshop at its pilot plant in Elche, Spain. Bringing together project partners both in person and online, the hybrid event provided a platform for scientific exchange, technological demonstrations and collaboration between academia and industry.

The workshop opened with a welcome and introduction by Dr Jaime García from GREENE, followed by a presentation of GREENE Enterprise's technologies for converting waste into circular fuels. Prof. Tomás Ramírez Reina from the University of Seville then presented research on catalyst design for circular fuels, highlighting the role of catalysis in developing more sustainable conversion pathways.

The programme also featured contributions from project partners. Gabriel de Freitas Batista from the University of Hohenheim presented work on the hydrothermal carbonization of wet feedstocks, focusing on process conditions and product quality, while Junhan Lu from Queen's University Belfast introduced research on integrated carbon dioxide capture and utilisation.

A key part of the day was dedicated to practical sessions and guided tours of GREENE's pilot facilities. Participants explored technologies related to pyrolysis oil and biomethane, as well as hydrogen, gaining insight into the industrial processes that complement the project's research activities.

The workshop also provided valuable opportunities for networking and knowledge exchange among partners, strengthening the links between scientific research, technological development and industrial application.

By bringing together complementary expertise across the consortium, the workshop reflects the collaborative approach at the heart of CIRCULAR FUELS: advancing the valorisation of biomass and waste towards more circular and sustainable fuels and energy carriers.
