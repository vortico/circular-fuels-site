Researchers and students working across different research initiatives at the [University of Seville](https://www.us.es/) recently came together for a scientific exchange meeting, bringing together members of the [CIRCULAR FUELS](https://circular-fuels.eu/) and [CLEVER FUEL](https://clever-fuel-lab.eu/) projects, alongside other students and early-career researchers.

The meeting provided an opportunity to share research results, discuss ongoing activities and exchange ideas, identifying potential synergies between different research lines. In particular, the close interaction between researchers involved in both projects highlights the value of sharing scientific knowledge, expertise and experimental resources.

This exchange also reflects the international dimension of CIRCULAR FUELS, a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Staff Exchanges project coordinated by the University of Seville. Gabriel de Freitas Batista, currently undertaking a research secondment in Seville from the University of Hohenheim (Germany), is contributing to this collaborative environment through his work with the local research team.

The collaboration with the University of Hohenheim will continue in December, when two researchers from the University of Seville are scheduled to undertake secondments at the German institution, further strengthening the exchange of knowledge and research connections between both universities.

These activities illustrate how international mobility and scientific interaction can foster lasting research collaborations and open new opportunities for joint work.
